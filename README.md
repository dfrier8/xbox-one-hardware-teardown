# xbox-one-hardware-teardown
Hands-on Xbox One hardware teardown, component identification, preventative maintenance, reassembly, and functional testing.
Project Overview

This project documents the complete disassembly, component identification, cleaning, reassembly, and functional verification of an original Xbox One.

The project provided hands-on experience working with computer hardware, internal cabling, storage devices, cooling components, wireless networking hardware, and safe electronics maintenance.

⸻

Phase 1 – Top Shell Removal

I began the teardown by carefully removing the Xbox One’s exterior enclosure.

* Released the plastic retaining clips without damaging the enclosure.
* Removed the top shell to access the internal chassis.
* Inspected the system for accumulated dust and debris.
* Documented the original condition before cleaning.



⸻

Phase 2 – Wireless Network Module

After opening the enclosure, I located and removed the wireless networking module.

* Identified the Xbox One’s wireless network board.
* Identified the coaxial antenna connection.
* Carefully disconnected the antenna and removed the wireless module.
* Preserved the module and connections for later reinstallation.

📸 Insert: Close-up photo of removed wireless networking module.

⸻

Phase 3 – Internal Chassis Access

I removed chassis screws C1–C8 and lifted the internal metal shielding to expose the major internal hardware.

Components identified included:

* 2.5-inch SATA hard disk drive (HDD) — internal system and game storage.
* Optical/Blu-ray disc drive — reads physical games and media.
* Cooling fan — provides airflow through the cooling system.
* Heatsink — dissipates heat generated by the processor.
* Motherboard — primary circuit board connecting the system hardware.
* Internal power and data cabling — connects storage, optical, and other internal components.

📸 Insert: Full internal overview showing the exposed components.

⸻

Phase 4 – Internal Component Removal

I continued the teardown by removing several major internal assemblies.

* Removed the 2.5-inch SATA HDD and mounting caddy.
* Disconnected and removed the optical/Blu-ray drive.
* Removed the front-panel/front I/O board assembly.
* Identified and maintained the associated power and data connections for reassembly.

📸 Insert: Photo showing the HDD, optical drive, and front-panel board removed and laid out.

⸻

Phase 5 – Cleaning & Preventative Maintenance

With the major components removed, I cleaned accumulated dust and debris from the system.

Cleaning included:

* Motherboard and internal chassis
* Cooling fan
* Accessible heatsink fins
* Ventilation openings
* HDD and mounting caddy
* Optical drive exterior
* Internal metal shielding
* Exterior enclosure

The heatsink remained installed after worn/contaminated fasteners began showing signs of stripping. Further removal was stopped to avoid unnecessary damage to the hardware.

📸 Insert: Photo of the cleaned components and cleaning equipment.

⸻

Phase 6 – Reassembly

After cleaning and inspection, I reassembled the Xbox One in reverse order.

* Reinstalled the HDD and mounting caddy.
* Reinstalled the optical drive.
* Reconnected SATA/data and power connections.
* Reinstalled the front-panel components.
* Reinstalled the internal metal shielding.
* Reinstalled the wireless networking module.
* Reconnected the wireless antenna.
* Reconnected the front-panel ribbon connection.
* Reinstalled and secured the exterior enclosure.

📸 Insert your three reassembly photos here in chronological order.

⸻

Phase 7 – Functional Verification

After completing reassembly, I powered on the Xbox One to verify successful operation.

The console successfully:

* Booted into the Xbox operating system.
* Connected to a 5 GHz wireless network.
* Established IPv4 and IPv6 connectivity.
* Recognized the reinstalled wireless networking hardware.
* Connected to Xbox network services.

The successful wireless connection also verified that the wireless networking module and antenna removed earlier in the teardown were properly reinstalled.
